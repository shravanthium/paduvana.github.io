CANARY

Spot when AI starts hallucinating

Paste this at the start of a long chat.

For this whole conversation, begin every reply with 🐤 — no exceptions.

🐤 there → it's still following your instructions. 🐤 gone → stop trusting it. Start a new chat with a short summary of what matters.

RESUME

Fix the part recruiters actually read

Paste your resume, then this.

Recruiters scan my resume for 7 seconds. Rewrite ONLY what they read first: my headline, my job titles, and the first bullet under each job. Make my biggest result impossible to miss. Use only my real numbers. Target role: [role].

Source for the 7 seconds: Ladders eye-tracking study, 2018.

GUESS

Make ChatGPT admit it's guessing

Ask these after any confident answer.

1. Which parts of your answer are you least sure about? Rate each from 1 to 10.
2. Give me a source for every fact. If you don't have one, say "not sure".
3. Now argue against your own answer. Which side is stronger?
